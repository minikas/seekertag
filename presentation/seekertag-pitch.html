The problem

A stranger may find your bag.
They still have no safe reason or simple way to return it.

THE GAP

Phone numbers expose owners. Bluetooth trackers locate objects, but do not create a private conversation or guarantee a promised reward.

SeekerTag

The solution

Scan. Talk. Return.

01

Tag what matters

Create an item, print its QR label as a PDF or write an NFC tag.

02

Open a private chat

Finders scan in the app with no account required. Chat and notifications keep contact details private.

03

Complete the handoff

The finder reviews a receiving address. The owner finishes the return and releases the reward from the conversation.

SeekerTag

Why Solana Mobile

The reward is real before the return.

OWNER

Reserve in SKR

SKR is selected by default. Choose an amount and term, then approve with Seed Vault Wallet.

SOLANA

Hold it in escrow

The funded reserve is visible. The owner cannot reclaim it before the chosen term ends.

FINDER

Receive payment

Review a Solana receiving address. After the handoff, the owner signs to release the reward.

Seeker sign-in and owner approvals use Mobile Wallet Adapter.
The finder can paste an address; connecting a wallet is optional.

Demonstrated on devnet with test SKR. SOL and USDC are also supported.
