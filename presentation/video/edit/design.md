# Smartphone as the subject

The real application sits inside one graphite Android phone. The frame has a continuous metal rim, rounded glass and restrained side buttons. It follows the geometry and material of the upstream `device-frame-stage` primitive, adapted to the recorded 9:20 screen. No iPhone notch or invented status bar.

Canvas 1920×1080, background #0D1615, text #F5FAF8, secondary #BCD0CA, accent #00BDCD. Keep the existing English caption style. Use Arial as an available system sans serif for supporting labels; the app retains its own recorded typography.

Focal element: physical phone and actual recorded controls. Edge anchors: concise chapter label at the top left, DEVNET at top right, captions at the bottom. Background is deliberately plain to keep attention on the functional demonstration; the phone's shadow and material supply depth.

Motion is functional. `spring-pop-entrance` gives the device a smooth 0.55-second arrival without overshoot. `coordinate-target-zoom` and `ui-focus-zoom` guide the camera to a real input or confirmation, hold long enough to read, and return to the full device. Camera moves transform the physical phone itself, not a duplicate image. Conversation uses one phone per person, with synchronized visual handoff between the real recordings.

Use the recorded Seed Vault Testuser verification and retain the on-screen distinction between that retake and the original return. Application success states come from the recordings.

## Production values

Complete palette, including graphite materials, shadow alpha and rail: #000, #0008, #00BDCD, #020604, #0D1615, #0c1110, #171e1c, #222b28, #222b2a, #304540, #394240, #5e6665, #64716d, #68736d, #BCD0CA, #F5FAF8, #ccd4d180. Supporting text uses Arial 500/600 at 18–29 px. Phone corners are 52 px outside and 40 px at the glass, with 12 px bezel padding. The handset has a restrained layered shadow. In the two-person scene, both phones remain fully in frame and the labels sit above them.
