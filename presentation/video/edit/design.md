# Smartphone as the subject

The real application sits inside one graphite Android phone. The frame has a continuous metal rim, rounded glass and restrained side buttons. It follows the geometry and material of the upstream `device-frame-stage` primitive, adapted to the recorded 9:20 screen. No iPhone notch or invented status bar.

Canvas 1920×1080, background #0D1615, text #F5FAF8, secondary #BCD0CA, accent #00BDCD. Keep the existing English caption style. Use Arial as an available system sans serif for supporting labels; the app retains its own recorded typography.

Focal element: physical phone and actual recorded controls. Edge anchors: concise chapter label at the top left, DEVNET at top right, captions at the bottom. Background is deliberately plain to keep attention on the functional demonstration; the phone's shadow and material supply depth.

Motion is functional. The device arrives once with a 0.4-second fade and 14 px settle, without overshoot. Later chapters keep a stable handset. The current edit keeps the whole phone in view. Native sheets, scrolling and keyboard transitions carry the movement. The coordinate-target-zoom support remains available in the generator, but this revision uses no camera zooms after visual review showed that following keyboard changes distracted from the action. Camera moves transform the physical phone itself, not a duplicate image. Conversation uses one phone per person, with synchronized visual handoff between the real recordings.

Use current SKR recordings and the current receiving-address form. A touch-indicator contact/compress/release/lift gesture lasts 0.25 seconds and sits over the actual pressed control; it never invents an application response. Application success states come from the recordings.

## Production values

Complete palette, including graphite materials, shadow alpha and rail: #000, #0008, #00BDCD, #00BDCD33, #020604, #0D1615, #0c1110, #171e1c, #222b28, #222b2a, #304540, #394240, #5e6665, #64716d, #68736d, #BCD0CA, #F5FAF8, #ccd4d180. Supporting text uses Arial 500/600 at 18–29 px. Phone corners are 52 px outside and 40 px at the glass, with 12 px bezel padding. The handset has a restrained layered shadow. In the two-person scene, both phones remain fully in frame and the labels sit above them.
